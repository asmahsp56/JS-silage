About JS Silage Wanda Factory

Powering Modern Dairy & Livestock with Highest Quality Nutrition

Situated strategically in the agricultural heartland of Hasilpur, JS Silage Wanda Factory (part of JS Feeds & Fodders, run under Dr. Abdul Basit) is a leading wholesale supplier of fermented whole-crop maize silage and balanced livestock wanda (concentrate feed).

Nutrient-Dense Corn Silage: Harvested at optimal dry matter stage with rich starch content and high grain ratio.

Precision Dairy Wanda: Formulated with protein meals, bypass fats, minerals, and vitamins for milking cows & buffaloes.

Uninterrupted 24-Hour Wholesaling: Trucks, trolleys, and freight vehicles can load at any hour of day or night.

Alfalfa Hay & Goat Wanda: Export-quality alfalfa hay and JS Special Wanda formulated specifically for goats.
